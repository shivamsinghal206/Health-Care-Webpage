Pediatric Health Tips

Health Tips for Parents

  • Ensure regular checkups to monitor growth and development.
  • Promote healthy eating habits with balanced nutrition.
  • Encourage physical activity and outdoor play.
  • Monitor screen time and promote quality sleep.
  • Teach children about emotional awareness and expression.
  • Create a safe and supportive home environment.
